Sight & Scene Places Near Bhubaneswar

Lingaraj Temple

Lingaraj temple is the main temple in Bhubaneswar and is a prime attraction for locals and tourists. The Lingaraj temple is a religious monument dedicated to Lord Shiva and is also one of the biggest temples in Odisha. The temple has eleventh-century architecture and is very old with a lot of significance. Lingaraja Temple is a Hindu temple dedicated to Shiva and is one of the oldest temples in Bhubaneswar, the capital of the Indian state of Odisha, India. The temple is the most prominent landmark of Bhubaneswar city and one of the major tourist attractions of the state.

Khandagiri & Udayagiri Caves

The Khandagiri and Udaygiri caves are also very famous among locals and guests in Bhubaneswar. Situated on the hills, the caves are ancient and have carvings and drawings that trail back to the Buddhist past of Kalinga. The murals of Khandagiri and the natural surroundings of the area enthrall you. Udayagiri and Khandagiri Caves, formerly called Kattaka Caves or Cuttack caves, are partly natural and partly artificial caves of archaeological, historical, and religious importance near the city of Bhubaneswar in Odisha, India.

Rajarani Temple

The Rajarani temple is also an 11th-century structure that is worth exploring for hours. The temple also has a greatly landscaped garden where you can rest and amaze by the intricate architecture of the Rajarani Temple. The Rajarani temple does not have any idols, but its architecture is enough to lure tourists to the magnificent temple. The temple is believed to have been known originally as Indreswara. It is locally known as a “love temple” because of the erotic carvings of women and couples in the temple. The temple was constructed of dull red and yellow sandstone locally called “Rajarani”. There are no images inside the sanctum, and hence it is not associated with a specific sect of Hinduism but is broadly classified as Saivite based on the niches.

Mukteswara Temple

The Mukteswara temple is among the many temples that make Bhubaneswar a temple city. There are many temples in Bhubaneswar, and Muketswara is one of the finest, with its distinct arch and its expansive courtyard. The temple is a 10th-century architectural example that people visit even today. There is also a beautiful garden and a pond in the compound where you can relax and take photographs. The temple’s arch is iconic and is featured in many important articles related to Bhubaneswar. It is one of the prominent tourist attractions of the city.
